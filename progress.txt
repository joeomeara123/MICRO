## 2026-01-11 | Interaction Complexity Matching

### Summary
- Refined UX model: swipe for binary, tap+expand+AI for complex tasks
- Added Gmail and Slack integration to MVP scope
- Expanded PRD list from 7 to 10 to cover new features

### Key Pattern: Interaction Complexity Matching
| Interaction | Task Type | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| **Swipe** | Binary (yes/no) | Approve contract, dismiss notification |
| **Tap → Expand → AI Assist** | Needs thought | Reply to email, respond to Slack |

### Card Flow for Complex Tasks
1. **Initial** - Minimal card in stack (title, source icon, priority)
2. **Expanded** - Full context shown on tap
3. **AI Prompt** - "Want me to draft a reply?"
4. **AI Draft** - Editable AI-generated response
5. **Action** - Send/Confirm button

### Key Principle
**User always stays in control.** AI offers to help, never auto-sends.
